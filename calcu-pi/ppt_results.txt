OpenMP PPT pi1/pi2/pi3/pi4 benchmark
======================================
Configuration:
  compiler: Apple clang with Homebrew libomp
  optimization: -O2
  NUM_STEPS: 1000000000
  NUM_THREADS: 16
  runtime threads: OMP_NUM_THREADS=16
  timing: omp_get_wtime(), includes the parallel computation and final reduction as in the PPT examples
  runs per program: 5

Compile commands:
  clang -O2 -Xpreprocessor -fopenmp -I$(brew --prefix libomp)/include -L$(brew --prefix libomp)/lib -lomp ppt_pi1.c -o ppt_pi1
  clang -O2 -Xpreprocessor -fopenmp -I$(brew --prefix libomp)/include -L$(brew --prefix libomp)/lib -lomp ppt_pi2.c -o ppt_pi2
  clang -O2 -Xpreprocessor -fopenmp -I$(brew --prefix libomp)/include -L$(brew --prefix libomp)/lib -lomp ppt_pi3.c -o ppt_pi3
  clang -O2 -Xpreprocessor -fopenmp -I$(brew --prefix libomp)/include -L$(brew --prefix libomp)/lib -lomp ppt_pi4.c -o ppt_pi4

The source files correspond to PPT pi1/pi2/pi3/pi4:
  pi4: reduction(+:sum)
  pi1: manual interleaved partition + sum[thread]
  pi2: omp parallel + omp for + sum[thread]
  pi3: private local sum + one critical update
  pi4: reduction(+:sum)

Summary (arithmetic mean of all 5 runs):
  pi1: 0.051750612 s
  pi2: 0.062559747 s
  pi3: 0.051092053 s
  pi4: 0.050635815 s

Summary (mean of runs 2-5, excluding first warm-up run):
  pi1: 0.048195005 s
  pi2: 0.053342461 s
  pi3: 0.049848259 s
  pi4: 0.048835754 s

Note: pi1/pi2 use a per-thread array updated inside the loop; pi3 uses a private local accumulator and one critical update per thread.
The first run is slower because of OpenMP runtime/thread startup and system noise.
